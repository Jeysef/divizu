Anti-shadowing layout

Every socket reserves the wider of its own notch and its child subtree on each side, so wide composites push neighbouring ports outward instead of covering them.

Physical metaphor

Drilled sockets are dependencies; bottom tabs are the implementations that plug into them. The drawing makes wiring mistakes obvious before runtime.

Deterministic output

Pure geometry and monospace width estimates produce the same SVG on every machine. No fonts to load, no browser engine, no random identifiers.
